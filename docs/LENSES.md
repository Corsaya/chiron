Status: deferred beyond the first SAT loop. Implements principles 1, 5, and 9 in [PHILOSOPHY.md](PHILOSOPHY.md).

A lens is a saved perspective over shared records. It defines included domains, visible summaries, and emphasis among suggestions. Life is the conceptual default perspective within existing navigation, not a new home screen.

Switching lenses does not duplicate data, modify evidence, change goals, or rearrange commitments. Important conflicts remain visible even when their domain is outside the active lens. Replanning is a separate explicit action.

Introduce Student, Athlete, and Builder perspectives only when real multi-domain usage justifies them. Verify that switching away and back preserves sessions, commitments, and evidence exactly.
