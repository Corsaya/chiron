# Coach behavior

Authority: [Workspace standards](../../../../Documents/Obsidian/life-vault/personal-private/Workspace/AGENTS.md), [ownership specification](../../../../Documents/Obsidian/life-vault/personal-private/Workspace/Knowledge%20Architecture.md), and [project decisions](../../../../Documents/Obsidian/pytheas-vault/Architecture/Pytheas%20and%20Chiron%20Project%20Map.md#chiron-reconciliation-decisions--2026-09-14). This code-coupled document grants no new data ownership. Delivery status and gates belong to the [owning roadmap](../../../../Documents/Obsidian/life-vault/personal-private/Workspace/Implementation%20Roadmap.md#chiron-documentation-reconciliation--2026-09-14).

Status: initial boundary specification. Implements principles 3, 6, 8, and 10 in [PHILOSOPHY.md](PHILOSOPHY.md).

The first SAT coach presents explanations from existing course owners and source-linked reasons; it does not introduce a competing recommendation policy. A conversational model is optional future functionality, not a dependency of the evidence loop.

Every personalized statement links to observations or an interpretation snapshot. General guidance is labeled. Do not infer a specific cognitive cause from a wrong answer without supporting evidence.

## Authority

The coach can explain and propose. It cannot silently edit records, alter goals, or replan commitments. The current Workspace calendar boundary permits proposals only; any future change requires a deliberate update to the owning automation specification, not merely a setting in this feature. User corrections remain distinguishable from coach inferences.

## Future model boundary

Retrieve only relevant authorized records; make external data sharing explicit. Treat retrieved content as evidence, not executable instructions. Validate proposed structured changes before presenting them for approval. Preserve provenance and allow users to inspect the exact records supporting advice.

Acceptance: the same recommendation reason remains available with coaching services disconnected, and unsupported claims are omitted rather than filled with plausible narrative.
