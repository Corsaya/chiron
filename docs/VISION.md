# Chiron's vision

**Chiron makes the next step clear, shows why it matters, and makes earned progress visible.**

Chiron is a Personal Intelligence System for Performance. It exists to help people turn the knowledge and experience they already have into useful action toward goals they choose.

It is for people learning, training, and building while balancing real commitments. SAT practice is the first proving ground: make one existing workflow useful enough to return to before expanding into other domains.

Chiron should reduce the effort of deciding what to work on, understanding recurring difficulties, and seeing what practice actually established. It connects evidence to a feasible next step through the user's existing workspace.

It should never decide a person's worth, manufacture certainty, or optimize engagement at the expense of their goals and rest. It should not require duplicate records, a replacement home screen, or surrendering control of personal commitments.

If every optional feature disappeared, this experience must remain: open the work you already own, understand a useful next action and its reason, do the work, and see an honest account of the result. Your records remain yours and remain usable independently of Chiron.

[Philosophy](PHILOSOPHY.md) defines the principles. [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md) describes how implementation follows the existing Workspace owners. This vision adds no new workflow or information authority.
